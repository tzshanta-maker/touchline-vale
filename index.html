Pick Mobile in Quick Match and choose the FC-style cluster or the DLS-like A/B/C pad; both are stored with the rest of your settings. Any USB/Bluetooth controller using the standard mapping works — just press a button to connect; a toast confirms it. The pad also drives the menus: D-pad or left stick to move the focus ring, A to select, B to go back, Start to kick off or pause. On touch devices, use the on-screen stick and the action buttons.

Match craft

  • Passing: aim assist picks the teammate in the direction you are running (or the right stick). Point at a teammate and press Z; with nobody there the ball goes into the space ahead.
  • First touch: players cushion the ball instead of letting it bounce off — better passers take a cleaner touch.
  • Skill move: double-tap Shift (or RB/RT) with the ball to spin through a challenge.
  • Shooting: the longer you hold C, the harder the shot — but power costs accuracy. Short taps are for close-range finishes.
  • Sprint smart: stamina drains fast and recovers slowly. Sprint to close down space, not to chase every ball.
  • Defending: press with the nearest player, then press E when the ball-carrier is within reach. Tackles work from any angle — the challenge goes in the direction you are running. A failed tackle leaves you flat-footed.
  • Goalkeepers dive on shots and rush out for loose balls inside the area. Keepers hold the ball for a moment before distributing.
  • Throw-ins: when the ball crosses a touchline, the nearest player of the other side takes it. Aim with the stick, cycle the highlighted receiver (X / THRU / RECEIVER) and throw (Z / PASS / THROW). There is no offside from a throw-in.
  • Corners and goal kicks: played automatically — you take control once the ball is live.
  • Auto tackle: hold Sprint while defending and your player chases the carrier; in range he challenges automatically (with a short cooldown). Manual tackles still work.
Matches are two halves. Your team attacks toward the far end of the pitch; the camera follows behind the play.
Squad & formations: every footballer has six attributes — pace, shooting, passing, dribbling, defending and physical — and they feed straight into the game: quick players win foot races, sharp shooters hit harder and straighter, strong passers find their man, good defenders tackle more often. Pick from seven formations on the title screen (or in the pause menu), open Squad to see the whole roster, and tired players are replaced automatically at the next stoppage — up to three substitutes per match.
